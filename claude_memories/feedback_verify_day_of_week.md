---
name: Verify Day of Week
description: Always verify the day of week from the date before stating it — don't guess or second-guess
type: feedback
---

When stating what day of the week it is, compute it from the date once and stick with it. Do not guess, do not flip-flop, do not second-guess after stating it.

**Why:** Repeatedly got the day of week wrong and kept changing answers when challenged, causing confusion about deadlines and scheduling.

**How to apply:** If you need to reference the day of the week, calculate it from the known date, state it confidently, and move on. If unsure, use `date` command in bash to verify before answering.
