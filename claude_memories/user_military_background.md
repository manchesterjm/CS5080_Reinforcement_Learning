## Family (Air Force Brat)
Father was Air Force — 6 years enlisted first, then entered the Bootstrap Program (AF program for enlisted to attend college and earn commission). Undergrad at Virginia Tech (Blacksburg), medical school at UVA (Charlottesville), commissioned as officer upon graduating med school.

**Father's assignments (Josh as dependent):**
- FE Warren AFB, WY — Josh born Jul 11, 1975
- AFB in Texas (~6 months, ~1975-76) — base no longer exists, possibly Webb AFB (Big Spring), closed 1977
- Blacksburg, VA — father's undergrad at Virginia Tech (brother Ben born 1977)
- Charlottesville, VA — father's med school at UVA (brother Andy born 1979), commissioned upon graduation
- Wright-Patterson AFB, OH (1981-82)
- Anderson AFB, Guam (1982-84) — family vacationed to Okinawa, Josh saw U-2s at Kadena
- Travis AFB, CA (1985-88)
- Wurtsmith AFB, MI (1988-92)
- Robins AFB, GA (1992-94) — Josh enlisted from here

**Siblings:** Ben (born 1977, Blacksburg), Andy (born 1979, Charlottesville)
